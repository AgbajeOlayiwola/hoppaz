# Hoppaz Brand, Content & Community System

Author: Jae (Jide Kelekun). Load at the start of any chat touching Hoppaz visuals, copy, the image library, the community, or strategy. Source of truth; do not freehand from memory. No em dashes in any Hoppaz copy or in replies to Jae.

## 1. What Hoppaz is
Hoppaz (always **H-O-P-P-A-Z**) is a **community-first movement in Lagos that runs a party bus**: community first, party second. Members are **Hoppers**. Never describe Hoppaz as just "a party bus"; the bus is one thing the community does. The community is a room of like minds (people who go out, build, create, grow) sharing what's good across nightlife, tech, music, food; everybody grows. The party product is the **Hop**: one orange bus, four secret stops in one night, the headline party is the last stop (grand finale), guaranteed ride back to the mainland. **Brand idea: the bus is the fifth party.** The venues are the excuse; the community forms on the bus between stops. House rule: **the bus never waits. Hop or stay.** Line: **come alone, leave with friends.**
Audience: 21 to 35, Lagos mainland + island, phone-first, burned by a 3am surge on Third Mainland Bridge. Lagos-street not Lagos-luxe, adult, not a kids' brand or a Lekki lounge. **Voice:** conductor not promoter: loud, cheeky, confident, short, second person ("Bus leaves at 7. Hop or stay."). No "experience", no first-line hashtags, no emojis unless Jae uses one first (bunny sign-off ok).

**Long-term vision (Jae):** rides evolve beyond Lagos into country trips at an accessible price (e.g. Lagos to Accra, Kigali, Burkina Faso), so Hoppers exist across cities: "Have you met a Hopper in another country?" Treat as a later phase, after Lagos Hops are proven and the community has a pulse. Never publish a trip price or route until real costs, visas and border rules for that exact route are verified. Lagos to Accra by road is the natural first country run.

## 4. Two named art styles (alternate across the feed)
Both are collage: four places cut out of Lagos, pasted into one night.
- **ROUGH CUT** (Style A, the default): flat brand-colour ground; halftone/tonal B&W photo cutouts with a thin cream keyline (black on cream ground); the tilted bounce wordmark; grain + paper wash + an orange "sun"; hand-scrawled marker accents; newsprint scraps; rough rubber-stamps; the route motif. Loud, night, DIY.
- **BROADSHEET** (Style B, the alternate): cream ground; upright flush-left Poppins Black (no tilt); flat colour blocks; photos as bordered orange+black **riso duotone** rectangles with slight ink misregistration; mono-caps captions; thin rules. Steadier hand, same soul.
(The original spec said "flat ground, no grain/paper". That has evolved: grain/paper/tonal treatments are now canon in Rough Cut, riso in Broadsheet. Flat brand colour still dominates; photo backgrounds only faint and behind, low opacity.)

### Collage-text techniques (headline type)
The three core headline treatments are below; the full named catalogue of lettering, cutout, colour and texture moves is in **Section 11 (Collage Playbook)**.
- **Sticker tiles** (the "HELLO TOKYO" reference look): each letter on its own bright rounded scrap, arced/overlapping, drop shadow; small circular badge letters for a second word; speech bubbles, stars, game-UI bits. Edges must be **rough** (SVG `feTurbulence`+`feDisplacementMap` tear filter), never neat.
- **Torn cut-paper (ransom)**: each letter an individually torn scrap: solid brand colour, photo-cut fill, some taped, mixed rotation/baseline.
- **Photo-filled letters**: Poppins Black with a Lagos photo via `background-clip:text` + black stroke + shadow; avoid one recognisable found face dominating a letter.

### Rules of thumb
One violet shape max (sun/moon, behind the subject); colour photo only as small punches (jollof, danfo-yellow, wristband, <10% each, max two) with orange staying loudest; texture from Lagos itself (newspaper, danfo signage, ticket stubs); one scrawl word max; route motif = signature (four cutouts, one line, ending at the finale) on every night flyer; one dominant cutout at 50 to 70% height, wordmark top or bottom third, never over a face. Don'ts: neat rounded shapes where rough is wanted, gradients, stock photos, a rabbit face, >4 colours, text over a face.

## 5. Build pipeline & sizes
HTML/CSS with Poppins loaded locally (via `npm pack @fontsource/*`; Google Fonts blocked by egress) → render Playwright deviceScaleFactor 2 → always eyeball & fix defects → run a Sonnet QC sub-agent on posters. Photo treatment order: desaturate → contrast → halftone/tonal → cut (background removal) → keyline. Reusable tooling in `scratchpad/cut.py`: `cutout`, `halftone`, `panel`, `punch`, `tonal` (preferred hero treatment: keeps greys + grain + fine dot screen), `tonal_panel`, `riso` (Broadsheet orange+black duotone). Sizes: 1080×1080 (feed / WhatsApp icons / IG highlight covers, **circular-safe**: keep content inside the inscribed circle, collage bleeds to corners), 1080×1350 (portrait poster, preferred), 1080×1920 (story). Export PNG 2×. Common defects: letters overlapping, keyline broken, orange not dominant, >1 violet shape, text over a face, low-contrast text over a photo.

### Catalogue rules
One row per raw image: `file | what is in it (every usable subject, comma separated) | location | quality note | cuts made`. e.g. `IMG_0412.jpg | danfo, Carter Bridge, CMS, crowd | CMS, Lagos Island | sharp, daylight | cuts/danfo-01.png`.
### Workflow, every time
1. Read `catalogue.md` first; never ask for an image already logged.
2. Decide what the piece needs; tell Jae in one line what is missing, subject by subject.
3. When Jae drops into `raw/`, open every image and log **everything** usable, not just the asked-for thing.
4. Cut needed subjects to `cuts/`, record them; cut extras only when cheap.
5. Build → save to `out/` → deliver → commit to the folder.
6. Update the catalogue before ending the turn. The catalogue is the memory, the chat is not.

## 7. Community model
Ladder: join → **Hopper**; ride a Hop → collect a **Badge** (digital stamp = proof of hop); **4 badges → Captain** (inner circle / bus captain). This is **status from the bus** and cannot be earned with app XP. App XP levels have their own names (proposed **JJC, Regular, Plug, Oga, Agba**, pending Jae), never Hopper or Captain, never "Bridge Rat", "Night Runner" or other "-er" job names. See Section 12. Main community **free**; inner tier = joining Hops. As the community grows beyond Lagos, Badges work as a passport (a country trip = a special stamp) and Captains are the natural city leads (Hoppaz Accra, Kigali, etc.).
**One WhatsApp Community** (umbrella, not separate communities): **Announcements** (broadcasts to everyone, the only guaranteed reach; keep low-frequency/high-signal; tickets + stop reveals here) · **Welcome** (locked read-only front door + router; pinned community-first welcome; members react to pick interest rooms) · **General / BASE** (open room, home base; keep it open and moderated, never locked) · **Rules** (locked house code: bus on time / no delays; security: verified riders, secret stops, look out for each other, zero-tolerance harassment; respect) · **Admin** (backstage) · **Hop groups** (one per hop; country trips reuse this template: one group per trip, deposit to join, closed after) · **Interest rooms** opened **on demand** via a reaction threshold (~25), never pre-built (ravers, live music, comedy, food crawls, tech & founders, run club, film, getaways; no religious, off-brand). Curated event lists are a **members-only perk inside** the community, not public content. Note: WhatsApp throttles rapid group-linking ("group linking temporarily disabled, try again in a few hours"); space out group creation, don't hammer retry.
**Open questions before launch:** (a) in WhatsApp Communities new members auto-join Announcements, not Welcome; test the landing flow with a spare number and make the first Announcements post route people to Welcome and General; (b) consider pinning the Hoppaz Code in Welcome instead of a separate locked Rules group, to avoid three locked rooms before anyone can talk; (c) require a volunteer host, plus the topic recurring in General, before an interest room opens; (d) City chapters only once a city has real members.
Group-icon set built (Rough Cut, circular-safe): Welcome · General/BASE · The Drop (announcements) · Rules · Admin · HOP 01 hop template (torn cut-paper is the leaning template; sticker-collage is the alt). Each future hop = a number/date swap.

### Competitor reference: Party Trotters
Lagos WhatsApp community (founder Ozioma Favour Onukogu, @OnukoguFavour), "life outside work" angle, almost no public footprint. Mostly forwards other organisers' flyers with member discounts/free tickets. Worth copying: the per-event area poll ("Who's coming? Where are you coming from?" split Mainland central / Gbagada / Ikeja / Lekki / Island; for Hoppaz this doubles as pickup planning), member intros (highest engagement), a pinned rules card (no spam, no DMs without permission, no event posts without organiser approval, respect, no bigotry). Their weak spots to beat: low turnout (3 yes vs 20 no on one poll), vendor/business joins, drift into generic networking, locked admin-only chat, no owned event. Hoppaz wins by owning the Hop, the Badge/Captain ladder, paid seats that convert interest into turnout, recaps, an open General, and a single weekly vendor plug thread.

## 8. The Hop (event product)
Board 6pm, **bus leaves 7pm and does not wait**. Four stops, last = grand finale, guaranteed ride home to the mainland, stops secret until the bus moves. **Cadence: every two weeks** (not shown on designs; the community/glossary carries it). Route: Stop 1 = mainland **gather/assembly** point (not an event; best anchor **New Afrika Shrine, Ikeja**: free, iconic, near the bridges) → island warm-up → comedy break → finale. **Finale = South Social**; third-stop candidate = **Laughter Cave** (comedy, Bogobiri, Ikoyi; confirm Friday availability). Free events skew island and rotate weekly; pull the week's listings ~5 days before each hop (nothingtodoinlagos substack, Eventbrite free filter). First Hop facts: ₦25,000 (bus + 4 stops + finale included), boarding 6 / leaves 7; tickets via **Chowdeck**; date moved out ~2 weeks from the original Fri 25 Sep 2026.

## 9. Growth & monetization (data-backed direction)
Lead ad spend at the **free community-join** (low friction, builds a retained re-marketable audience = the compounding asset), not the one-off ticket, but also run a thin direct "next hop" fill so buses never sit empty. Optimize for **cost per member who becomes a paying rider**, not cost per join (a free list is a vanity trap). Test the two ad types **in parallel**, equal budget; judge on downstream spend/repeat, not day-one CPC. **Cold-start rule (most important): don't run ads until the community has a pulse.** Seed 50 to 100 real people + chatter + recap photos first; a dead room = instant churn. Instrument invite links per source (IG bio, ad, status, flyer QR) or the test is unmeasurable. Referral loop: "bring your people" → badge/discount (cheapest channel). **Monetization sequence:** (1) ticket margin now; (2) venue footfall deals after proof ("we bring 40 to 60 spenders"; first hops free/paid-to-venue, then flip to charging); (3) drink commissions (secondary, messy); (4) brand ads to the community + sponsorship, scale-stage only, sparingly (overuse poisons the room). Segment interest rooms only when overflowing; the per-vertical curated event list is a Phase-3 second business; country trips are also a later phase. Don't split focus early. Later revenue lines (see Section 12): Hoppaz for organisers (claimed event pages, then a ticket pledge, then native ticketing on Bachs with a founding organiser rate) and brand quests (brands fund the rewards).

## 11. Collage Playbook (named techniques: build by name, pick the best)
Reverse-engineered from the 14 reference posters (the `REF-` style board). Call a move by its name and build it. Full detail + CSS per technique lives in `out/HOPPAZ-COLLAGE-PLAYBOOK.md` in the library.
**How to pick ("pick the best"):** one dominant technique per piece, two max; match the technique to the message (loud launch = sticker tiles or spray scrawl; a hop in a series = episode numeral; an editorial/community piece = Broadsheet + boxed-quote; a recap = hero-vehicle or reaching-crowd). Always a separation device around a cutout (keyline / hard shadow / misregistration ghost); keep a 10:1+ scale ratio headline-to-fine-print; let photos carry the chroma and keep flat saturated colour to 2-3 placements that form a triangle; leave 20-40% open (usually the top) for the wordmark.

**Text / lettering:** Sticker-tile arc (built) · Torn ransom (built) · Photo-filled letters (built) · **Spray-marker scrawl** (one-colour marker headline over a B&W halftone crowd; feTurbulence wobble + 2px blur halo + ~80 speckle dots + 3-4 drips; writes over torsos) · **Knocked-out stamp word on a torn black plate** (cream word knocked out of a #0E0B0A rectangle with a torn bottom `clip-path` + tear filter; tiny typewriter strapline above) · **Heavy condensed title + boxed typewriter quote** (Anton/Bebas 0.88 line-height + a cheeky line in a mono box tilted ~1deg; good for Rules/glossary) · **Episode numeral** (giant cream geometric number tucked behind the hero cutout via z-index = the Hop 02/03/04 series system) · **Rotated orange sticker-plate headline** (lowercase heavy extended grotesk on a flat orange rect rotated ~-6deg, pinned over a cutout corner) · **Gradient-fill title + texture-type** (gradient word + a narrow column of tiny pidgin/route-code type as texture) · **Circled hand-drawn date** (marker date inside a wonky open SVG ellipse) · **Chip-whisper** (small cream badge letters over a huge word) · **Tiny app-UI corners** (nav row + Lagos coords N 6.5244 / E 3.3792 + circled TM; loud art + precise UI reads expensive).

**Collage / cutout:** Die-cut keyline single/double (built) · **Single-ink coarse halftone hero + violet misregistration ghost** (coarse 4-6px dot screen on flat ground, cream keyline, violet copy offset 6px behind at ~60%) · **1-bit bitmap halftone over a flat block** (heavy threshold, `mix-blend-mode:multiply` so the orange block shows through whites) · **Object passes through the title** (z-index split, hero weaves between two headline copies) · Sun-disc halo behind crowd (built) · **Danfo-as-plinth** (bus as stage, 3x cutouts rising from its roof over a tiny crowd, on cream) · **Hands-as-scale-joke** (giant halftone hand cupping a tiny bus + tiny crowd) · **Offset colour-swatch backdrop** (3-4 misaligned orange/violet/cream rects peeking from behind the hero) · **Colour-block duotone panels + stripe overlay** (rects holding greyscale photos in multiply/screen with a repeating-linear-gradient ankara/bus-seat stripe) · **One colour-pop in a mono crowd** · **Hero vehicle on flat ground, cropped mid-drive** (bus of passengers on flat orange, flat ground-shadow ellipse, cropped off one edge) · **Reaching-crowd hands from the bottom edge** · **Hard-cropped baseline plinth + caption columns** ("a bus of / loud nights", "a bus of / bad decisions").

**Colour combos (brand-swapped):** orange ground + B&W halftone + one violet pop (core) · cream ground, photos carry all chroma · 3-spot screenprint (one hot ground + black halftone + cream keyline, no gradients) · violet ground + orange tile triangle + black/grey · warm mono field + one cool surprise (orange field, one violet-gradient skyline) · riso triad orange/ember/black on cream.

**Composition:** totem/pyramid stack on a vertical axis with landmarks flanking · diagonal hero vs orthogonal blocks · faint 28px graph-paper grid behind the top two-thirds fading down · radial satellites around a central face · numbered episode system.

**Texture / finish:** coarse halftone dot-screen (crosshatch linear-gradients or SVG) · misregistration ghost (offset violet copy) · tear/erode filter (built) · folded-kraft-paper ground (noise + two 1px crease lines V+H @12% + scuff) · grain (built) · scanline/riso banding on the sun · spray fuzz + drips + speckle.

**Status:** already in the toolkit: sticker tiles, torn ransom, photo-filled, keyline, the sun, grain, rubber-stamp, tear filter, route motif. Highest-value to pull in next: the **episode numeral** (Hop series), spray-marker scrawl, hands-as-scale-joke, colour-block duotone panels, the misregistration ghost, the single-ink halftone hero.

## 12. The game layer, brand quests and the app (summary)
Full detail lives in **Hoppaz Game Plan v3** (`docs/Hoppaz-Game-Plan.md` in `jaethecreator/hoppaz`, branch `ui-refresh`) and the app spec `docs/UI-REFRESH-PLAN.md`. Read those before planning game or app work. Summary of what was decided with Jae on 8 Oct 2026:
- **Game loop (Game Plan):** open a box daily, collect Lagos (the card deck), show it off, go outside for more, meet people at Hops. Money never buys boxes, cards or odds; it can only buy back a streak (18+).
- **Rank:** bus status (Hopper, then Captain at 4 Hop badges) is canon and cannot be ground out with XP. XP levels have separate names (proposed JJC, Regular, Plug, Oga, Agba).
- **Brand quests (the differentiator, after the UI launch):** organisers and brands set quest lines and fund the rewards; Hoppaz builds the quest line, verifies actions, tracks referrals for events hosted on Hoppaz, can power ticket rewards, and adds XP and badges that carry across organisers. Pre-event (going, follow on X or Instagram, like or repost, referral link, bring your crew), at the event (verified check-in, sponsor QR, organiser photo tasks such as "snap pictures with four people" or "snap the DJ", post tagging the brand), post-event (recap photo after check-in, rate the night, going to the next one, merch). Examples: Blockfest pre-event quests, South Social on-the-night photo tasks. Model comes from Jae's questing at Absinthe Labs: X checks through the X API, Instagram by screenshot reviewed by AI (a cheap Groq vision model) with a human review queue. Research found no ticketing platform offering this (only referral cashback such as Posh Kickback, Shotgun Cashback, Tixr Rewards; conference quest apps; one-off festival builds like Coachella Quests 2024), and nothing in Africa.
- **Quest guardrails:** real rewards only for verified actions, capped per person; shares and invites earn XP only; brands deposit reward codes or stock before a quest goes live; screenshot checks need handle match, freshness, duplicate-image detection, spot checks and re-checks; fixed rewards for actions, because prize draws in Nigeria need a National Lottery Regulatory Commission permit.
- **Hoppaz for organisers:** (A) claimed event pages with lineup ("Harry Obi, on the panel"), updates and Follow, plus a claim campaign ("Are you a brand or organiser? Claim your event on Hoppaz. DM us."); (B) ticket pledge: organisers give tickets (3 minimum) that power quests and box prizes, pilotable now with the existing drop system; (C) native ticketing on a Hoppaz ticket layer over **Bachs** payments (Jae knows the owner; Connect sub-accounts, organiser collects, Hoppaz platform fee). Pricing starts cheap: a founding organiser rate (e.g. 2.5%, locked 6 to 12 months for the first 20 to 50 organisers, end date announced) then 4.5%. Pitch instant naira payouts. Clear with Bachs in writing: its "mystery boxes" prohibition vs Hoppaz's free boxes, that ticketing is allowed, licence, local card cap.
- **Card of the Day:** only signed, place or public-domain cards are featured; unsigned cards appear only as locked silhouettes with no name or likeness ("A Grammy Club card is waiting for its artist. Is it you? DM us."). Never use a living person's name or likeness in content or ads without sign-off.
- **In the app:** a streak chip on the map (drawn flame icon, never emoji) opens "Ways to earn"; event cards carry their quests and the reward for going; Me shows the level name, bus status with badge stamps, two big numbers (daily streak and XP) and "Ways to earn"; badges are stamped ticket stubs; motion is a 180ms stamp, star flights only for badges, rank changes and drops; one four-sealed-box reveal screen serves partner drops now and the daily box later. Theme follows the Lagos clock (cream by day, Night Black after sunset). Mascot only at first run, empty states, reveals, badge and rank moments, errors, the report card, and small on Me; never on the map, event lists or chat.
